Why clients keep referring Kate

Kate opened Harbourline Home Loans in 2014 after eight years inside two large banks. She saw too many Sydney families turned away by processes, not by real risk. Her brokerage works the other way round. Every application is read, prepared and argued case by case before it goes to a lender.

Today she and her small team have settled more than $480 million in home loans for first buyers, refinancers and investors across Greater Sydney. Kate answers her own mobile during a transaction. If a lender asks for one more document, she calls instead of emailing. That habit is why 212 Google reviews average 4.9 stars and why most new clients arrive by referral.

Outside the office she coaches her daughter's under-12 rugby team and judges the Parramatta local business awards.
